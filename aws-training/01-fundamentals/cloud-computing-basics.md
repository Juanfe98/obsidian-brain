## Cloud Computing 

Cloud Computing is the on demand delivery of IT resources and applications through the internet. 

## Key benefits of using cloud computing

- Programmable resources
- Dynamic Abilities
- Pay as you go

| Cloud Services Provider                                                                                          | Traditional on-premise                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| With cloud services, the infrastructure (Physical hardware) is taken care of by the cloud vendor. (E.g AWS). See [[aws-infrastructure]] for details on how AWS organizes its global infrastructure.     | You figure out how much capacity you need, purchase de hardware, wait for it, set it up, and access the resources over your network. |
| Resources are virtualized, and you can access them through the internet.                                         | If there is not enough capacity, you must purchase more and wait for the servers to be delivered to your site.                       |
| You determine when and how often you need them so your interactions are configured based on your business needs. | If you have too much capacity, you are left with an over-provisioned environment.                                                    |
| You have the control to customize your storage, business applications and configure the entire network.          |                                                                                                                                      |
