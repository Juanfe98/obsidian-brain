## Interview Questions

Ask about past behavior, not hypothetical interest.

1. Tell me about the last time you experienced [problem].
2. What did you do?
3. What made that hard or frustrating?
4. What have you tried before?
5. Why did or didn't that work?
6. How often does this happen?
7. What does it cost you in time, money, risk, or frustration?
